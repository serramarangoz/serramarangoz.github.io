The frame. Solid walnut. Front leg and arm meet in one A-shape that carries the load straight to the floor. Glued mortise-and-tenon joints, no metal brackets.

Webbing. Elastic straps woven at right angles across seat and back. The first thing that gives when you sit down.

Foam. Blocks laid on the webbing and wrapped in a thin fibre layer, so the cushion edges stay round and full.

Fabric. Stretched and sewn by hand, the weave running unbroken from back to seat. Porto ships fully assembled.
